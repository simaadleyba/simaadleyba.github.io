Selected Research

2026

Who Belongs Together? Topical and Social Structure in Bluesky Starter Packs ↗

Sima Adleyba and Onur Varol. Preprint on arXiv:2609.31297.

ICWSM 2027: under reviewNetSciX: submitted

We examine whether human-curated starter packs capture meaningful topical and social groups. Semantic and network analyses show coherent communities, alongside limited coverage of members' wider audiences.

Computational social science, social networks, human curation

2025

Traffic Accident Severity Prediction ↗

CS512 course project at Sabancı University, Oct 2025 to Jan 2026

Predicted injury severity in U.S. traffic crashes with class-weighted models and examined results using SHAP, LIME, and DiCE. With Başak Çarhacıoğlu and Ahmet İhsan Gül.

Machine learning, explainability

2024–25

Kariyer.net Knowledge Base ↗

Industry project with Kariyer.net, Oct 2024 to Jun 2025

Built a skill extraction and normalization pipeline for Turkey's largest job platform using LangChain, GPT-4o-mini, and HDBSCAN. The resulting knowledge graph contains over 114,000 nodes and roughly 61 million edges.

NLP, knowledge graphs, LLMs
